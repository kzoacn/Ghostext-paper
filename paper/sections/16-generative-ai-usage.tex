\section{Generative AI Usage}
\label{sec:genai-usage}

Generative AI tools assisted code writing, LaTeX editing, paper drafting,
experimental analysis, proof development and checking, and review of this
revision. The earlier draft records use
of Codex and Claude Code. This revision also uses an independent AI reviewer to
identify weaknesses; that review is an internal quality check and does not
predict a program committee decision. The human authors remain responsible
for checking the complete manuscript, code, references, and measurements before
submission and for completing the S\&P 2027 Generative AI usage considerations
field. Completion of that human review is not asserted by this working draft.

No generative AI tool was used to fabricate results, alter raw measurements, or replace author judgment on security claims. All reported quantitative results come from project scripts and released result files.
