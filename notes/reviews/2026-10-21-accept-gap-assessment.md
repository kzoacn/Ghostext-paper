Independent gap assessment for an Accept-level revision

Date: 2026-10-21, Asia/Shanghai. This assessment preserves both previous reviews and does not change their **Weak Accept** recommendation. A higher target is not new evidence.

I reread the frozen contribution, stopping analysis, proof appendix, limitations, and evidence ledger, and inspected the upstream RRC core and changelog. The scientific baseline remains the PDF with SHA-256 `d59bbd0daaa94981a6a08f3d4f81bad60b265f3c240c49821860f5117638dd41` and archive with SHA-256 `a049f07125eada70d943af2758dbffcd9d49802a65b77852a91dd9f801e09cc0`. The current documentation commit is `9b57042`; the reviewed scientific freeze was `1c25344`. No paper/code edits or model experiments were performed for this assessment.

There is no outstanding artifact blocker to fix into an Accept. The remaining question is whether the stopping result becomes a sufficiently general design insight or a sufficiently consequential external-system finding. Three gaps matter most.

1. **The main theorem has a narrow structural reach.** The exact likelihood is already general, but the growing-separation theorem only instantiates a fixed nonuniform binary IID source. Random-walk stopping and phrase-length concentration have a substantial prior literature; enlarging the alphabet or restating a standard limit theorem would not materially strengthen the contribution. [Drmota, Reznik, and Szpankowski](https://www.reznik.org/papers/TIT10_tunstall_khodak.pdf).

   A promising extension is a terminal-surprisal criterion: if an encoder's final surprisal concentrates within a window of width `w_B=o(sqrt(B))`, while the independent natural source at the relevant lengths has vanishing probability of occupying such a window, then exact matching of the length marginal still cannot hide the encoder. This is a candidate research direction, not a result established by this note. The event inequality alone is elementary. The substantive work must establish its premises for a useful stopping family, give finite-length or asymptotic bounds, and derive a design implication such as the amount of additional information spread required to defeat this mechanism.

   **Falsifiable target:** prove the result for at least one genuinely dependent source with explicit hypotheses and a verified positive asymptotic information variance; compare against a zero-variance control. A Markov chain whose rows are permutations of the same probability vector may leave the surprisal increments IID and would be a cosmetic extension. Use distinct row entropies and check the resulting dependence. If verification-dependent stopping is covered, prove its terminal-concentration premise instead of importing the midpoint hazard. State how cap/failure outcomes enter the comparison. Exact small-state enumeration should agree with the theorem and its exceptions. Positive entropy alone is not sufficient.

2. **The strongest external result stops short of current verification-enabled software.** The present critique correctly addresses the published RRC algorithm. The separate verified adapter and three selected repairs do not establish what the current upstream implementation emits. The upstream now checks reverse recovery before accepting a stop; its default core therefore warrants a separate experiment. [Upstream changelog](https://github.com/ryehr/RRC_steganography/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), [upstream core](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ryehr/RRC_steganography/main/rrc_core.py).

   **Falsifiable target:** pin an immutable upstream revision and execute its actual encoding/extraction functions with verification enabled and disabled, sharing experimental inputs where appropriate. Validate the verification branch on prespecified small fixtures and retain every real-model attempt. Compute the public score from that implementation's actual interval masses. It uses float64 cumulative boundaries converted to Decimal and rotations from `prng.random()`; the artifact's quantized-source/192-bit-grid theorem does not transfer unchanged. Record numerical precision, normalization, rotation source, caps, tokenization failures, and independent receiver recovery.

   The decisive observation would be a reproducible stopping signal in the verification-enabled external implementation after exact length matching, including successfully recovered texts. If verification removes the signal, that is an informative boundary result and must narrow the practical claim. Selecting only examples where verification preserves the attack would not close this gap.

3. **The empirical follow-up is still exploratory and lacks a mechanism-specific real-model control.** The existing complete expansion cohorts are useful, but they reuse the original small topic split after changing the source policy. Additional seeds on those topics cannot by themselves establish transfer. The uniform-IID negative control checks theory; it does not check the real-model scoring pipeline under an endpoint intervention.

   **Bounded confirmatory design:** freeze the source, algorithms, score, eligibility rules, and analysis before generation. Use 20 fresh, independently selected prompt clusters per model, two payload sizes, and four secrets/tapes per cell: 160 secret instances per model, each evaluated under the paired stopping variants. One model is a useful first stage; a second family gives a maximum of 320 secret instances for the proposed empirical route. These are design suggestions, not authorization to start those jobs. Use independent natural content with the exact corresponding length histogram, report all generation/recovery outcomes, and resample prompt clusters rather than individual repeated texts.

   Add an exogenous-length control using the same token source and an endpoint independent of sampled content. For an ideal active sampler this should remove the stopping factor before conditioning on delivery; show that fact analytically or by exact enumeration, then use it to check the measurement pipeline. A non-significant AUC alone does not prove equality. Delivery overflow and retry conditioning must remain visible, since discarding unsuccessful fixed-budget cases can recreate selection. This control can share the confirmatory study; it need not become another large benchmark.

My decision criteria are alternatives, not an instruction to accumulate every possible experiment:

- **Theory route:** a correct general termination result with a substantive dependent-source instantiation, an explicit exception, and a useful efficiency/randomization consequence would remove the main contribution-depth reservation. The premises must be proved for the claimed algorithm class, and the finite checks must match. A wrapper around the existing binary proof would leave my rating unchanged. Under this route, present-day upstream vulnerability need not be claimed, and a large new model study is not necessary.
- **External-system route:** if the theorem extension is modest, a pinned, verification-enabled upstream result on two model families, fresh prompts, independent receiver checks, and the endpoint control would provide the missing practical weight. A proposed advance criterion is an AUC of at least 0.75 with its prompt-cluster 95% lower bound above 0.5 at both payload sizes in both models, alongside disclosed failures and a control consistent with the established null mechanism. This is my suggested minimum useful effect for this study, not a venue rule or a reason to tune the experiment after observing results. A small significant effect, or a signal confined to failed recovery, would not meet that criterion.

Either route would justify a fresh assessment for **Accept**, provided the new claim survives independent checking and the paper is centered on that contribution. A negative or inconclusive result should be reported and the scope revised; do not keep adding runs until a threshold is crossed.

Unknown message length, unknown prompts, model mismatch, ordinary-EOS deployment, human text quality, a low-FPR operational detector, and a fully secure replacement protocol are optional breadth for this paper. Known length and a known source are legitimate assumptions for the stated counterexample. More model names, more formatting work, or more passing regression tests do not address the remaining contribution gap. The priority is a stronger general result or a consequential external validation, not a broader list of measurements.
